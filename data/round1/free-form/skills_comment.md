## AI-generated summary

Round-1 respondents converge strongly on two themes: prompt engineering as a core new skill, and a shift in emphasis from manually writing/documenting requirements toward critically validating, reviewing, and governing AI-generated output (including recognizing hallucinations and bias). Many answers frame this as requirements engineers moving up the value chain toward stakeholder communication, negotiation, and business/domain understanding, while AI absorbs routine drafting work. A recurring caveat is that human judgment and domain expertise must remain central and "in charge," with several respondents explicitly warning against over-reliance on AI output.
