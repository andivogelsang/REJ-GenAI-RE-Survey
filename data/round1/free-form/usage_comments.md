## AI-generated summary

Across all RE activities, respondents most often described using GenAI for text-centric generation and transformation tasks: drafting or reformulating requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria; generating diagrams and models (frequently PlantUML/UML); and summarizing or extracting information from documents, interviews, and transcripts. In elicitation, GenAI was mainly used to prepare interview questions, create personas, and analyze raw stakeholder input. Analysis and validation responses centered on consistency/conflict checking, generating test cases and checklists, and flagging quality issues, while several respondents noted still needing to manually verify AI output. Management-related uses were comparatively sparse and mostly about summarization, prioritization, and traceability support.
